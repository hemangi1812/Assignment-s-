4. Write down the steps (as an algorithm, using simple numbered instructions) for ordering food on Swiggy — from opening the app to completing payment.

Algorithm for Ordering Food on Swiggy Application

Step 1: Start.
Step 2: Open the Swiggy app.
Step 3: Log in/sign-up to your account .
Step 4: Add or use your current location for your delivery.
Step 5: Search for a restaurant or food item you want to order.
Step 6: Select the restaurant/ specific food.
Step 7: Choose the food items you want.
Step 8: Add the selected items to the cart.
Step 9: Review the cart and check the total amount.
Step 10: Click on Proceed to Checkout.
Step 11: Add or confirm the delivery address and add additional information(call, text or leave on doorstep).
Step 12: Choose a payment method (UPI, Card, Cash, etc.).
Step 13: Complete the payment.
Step 14: Receive the order confirmation.
Step 15: Track your order until it is delivered.
Step 16: Stop.
